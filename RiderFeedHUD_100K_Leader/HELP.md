# WOW 100K Leader & Sweeper HUD — Quick User & Controls Guide

## 1. One-Click Installation & Reload
* Double-click **`install_RiderFeedHUD_100K.bat`** (Windows) or **`install_RiderFeedHUD_100K.command`** (Mac).
* In Sauce for Zwift, click your open HUD window and press **`Ctrl + R`** (or enable **WOW 100K Leader HUD v16** under Sauce Settings -> **Windows**).

## 2. Leading & Sweeping the D WOW 100K (`2.00 W/kg` & `37 kph`)
* **Top-Left (`100K WKG: 2.00`):** Tracks your live W/kg and cumulative 100K ride average (`Avg X.XX`). Use `−` / `+` to adjust target W/kg in `0.05` steps or click `Rst` at the start banner.
* **Top-Right (`100K | 2.00 | 37kph`):**
  - **Emerald Green (`FRONT OK` / `GROUP OK`):** Group is holding `<= 2.00 W/kg` on the front and cruising near `37 kph`.
  - **Flashing Red (`EASE!`):** You have pushed above `2.08 W/kg` on the front—ease slightly so the D pack stays compact.
  - **Click `100K` (`XXK left`):** Zeroes the 100.0 km distance counter as you cross the start banner.
* **Pinning Leaders (`LDR`) & Sweeps (`SWP`):**
  - Click **`LDR`** on any rider row (or type their name in `Co-Ldr…`) to highlight them in **Beacon Yellow** and track them in the left radar card.
  - Click **`SWP`** on any rider row (or type their name in `Sweep…`) to highlight them in **Vibrant Red** and track their gap and regroup status in the middle radar card.
  - Click **`Stg 5m`** on the right `🚂 B/C` radar card to cycle the staggered start offset (`5m` / `10m` / `15m` / `Live Only`) for chasing Cat B & C groups.

## 3. Top Black Bar & `⚙️` Settings Controls
* **`⚙️` Settings Cogwheel:** Adjust **Text Font Size**, **UI Scale**, **Background Opacity**, **256 Pantone Colours** (or **🟦 Screenshot Blue**), **Compact/Reset Columns**, **Paged View (`📄 Paged`)**, and **Top Bar Pin (`📌`)**.
* **`⛶` Full Screen (`F11`) / `_` Minimize / `✕` Exit:** Maximize to full screen, collapse the table to show only the top 100K banners, or close the HUD window.
