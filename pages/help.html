🔄 Multi-Lap "Real Pass" vs. "Lapped" Badges (30+ Lap Watopia 150)

• SAME LP — Total Zwift eventDistance matches yours (±2.25 km). Genuine Real Pass!
• +1 LAP — Faster group is +5 km / +10 km ahead in event distance (already passed you earlier). Lapping you — keep steady pace, do NOT drop fence or surge!
• -1L UNLAP — Late-starting faster pen is still 1+ lap behind you in total distance. Physically unlapping, not the real pass yet!
• ⤵️ -1L — Lapped straggler 1+ lap behind in distance. Automatically ignored by your 🧹 SWP Sweep Radar!

❤️ Age-Calibrated Heart Rate Spectrum & Zone 5/6/7 Redline Formula

• Max HR Formula: Computed automatically per rider from their Zwift Age: Max HR = round(208 - 0.7 × Age) (e.g., Age 68 → 160 bpm).
• 256-Colour HR Bar: Full-height bar on your row scales smoothly from 55 bpm (Deep Blue) through Cyan, Emerald, Gold, and Orange to Max HR (Dark Crimson Red).
• Zone 5/6/7 Redline Timer (RED 0-120s): Triggers and flashes red strictly in Zone 5/6/7 (default >= 90% of Age Max HR, e.g., >= 144 bpm at Age 68).
