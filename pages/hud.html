❓ Visual Field Guide & How to Lead a 100K TTT

🚧 Side-by-Side C & B Chaser Radar + Fence-Off Alert (800-Rider Edition)

• Always-Visible C & B Side-by-Side: For Cat D Leaders, the Right Radar Box permanently displays C & B's exact Distance & Time Behind side-by-side (e.g. C: -0.52mi (3m45s) | B: -1.85mi (9m)) and is never hidden by +1L lappers!
• 4-Stage Fence-Off Workflow: (1) 5m Out: Amber Heads-Up → (2) 90s Out: Flashing 🚧 FENCE OFF! warning so you can disable the D Fence before C/B arrives → (3) Passing: 🔓 FENCE OFF (JOIN C) while the blob flows past → (4) -28s Ahead: Green ✅ CLEAR confirmation!
• Instant Fence-Controlled Group Lock: Because all WOW groups (A–E) ride behind an active Fence with no solo breakaways, locks immediately onto the very first Cat C / B / A rider or Yellow Beacon entering nearby!
• Auto Pen-Exit Start Reset (0.05 mi — Zero Buttons to Click!): Zwift zeroes official event distance at the pen gate, and v21.7 automatically resets your Ride-Average W/kg (Rst → Auto✓) as you roll past 0.05 mi (80m) so pen warm-up spinning never skews your ride average!

💯 Monthly 100-Mile & Watopia 150 (32 Laps): Real Pass vs. Lapped Guide

1. Click 💯 100M/150 on Row 2: Sets target distance to 100.0 Miles (160.9 km) (~31.8 Lightning Laps after 1.8K lead-in; earns the 30-Lap Watopia 150 badge at 94.6M / 152.2K and finishes at 100.0M!), switches units to MI / mph (click MI/KM anytime to toggle), and activates the 🔄 5.0K Lp circuit filter.
2. How Real Pass vs. Lapped Works Automatically (Uses Zwift Event Distance):
• [SAME LP] + 🚨 REAL PASS: Their total event distance matches yours (±2.25 km). Cat C/B/A is genuinely catching your group on the same lap (~60K / 37.3M)!
• [+1 LAP] + 🔄 LAPPING (HOLD): Group is +5 km / +10 km ahead of you in event distance. They already passed you earlier—hold steady pace, do NOT surge!
• [-1L UNLAP] + ⏳ UNLAPPING: Faster Cat A/B started >8m behind you and is still 1+ lap behind in total distance—overtaking to unlap, not a real pass yet!
• [⤵️ -1L] Lapped Straggler: Dropped rider 1+ lap behind in distance—Sweeps (SWP) automatically ignore lapped riders so you never drop back by mistake!

👑 Step-by-Step: How to Set Up the Grid to Lead a WOW 100K TTT (60 Seconds)

1. Select 100K Mode & Distance: On Row 2, click 👑 100K TTT and verify 100K is highlighted under KM:.
2. Click Your Category (A–E): On the Left Green Card, click A (3.5–4.5w / 44kph), B (2.7–3.3w / 41kph), C (2.1–2.6w / 39kph), D (1.6–2.0w / 37kph), or E (1.2–1.5w / 33kph).
3. Lock Yourself in the Middle: Click 🎯 YOU on the right side of Row 2 so your row stays locked dead-center even in a 300-rider pen.
4. Tag Your Co-Leader & Sweeps: Click LDR on your Co-Leader's row (turns Beacon Yellow) and click SWP on your Sweeper's row (turns Vibrant Red), or type their names into the Co-Ldr… and Sweep… boxes.
5. Concertina the Columns: Drag any vertical bar (||) in the table header (or click Compact Cols inside ⚙️ Settings) to tighten column spacing.
