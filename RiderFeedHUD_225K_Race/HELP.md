# Quick Race Guide — 22.5 KM & 209m Climbing HUD v2.0

* **One-Click Install:** Double-click `install_RiderFeedHUD_Race.bat`, then click your open HUD window in Sauce and press **`Ctrl + R`**.
* **Adjusting 1m / 5m / 20m W/kg or Course Distance:** Click **`−` / `+`** on the top cards or click **`⚙️`** in the top-right corner to type new values for `1m`, `5m`, `20m`, `km`, or `m`.
* **Zeroing Distance & Climbing at the Start Banner:** Click the **`0K`** button on the far right of the 4th card as you cross the start line to zero the `22.5K` and `209m` counters.
