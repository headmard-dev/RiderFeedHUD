# Zwift 22.5K Open Race & Climb HUD v7.0 — Quick Race Guide

## 1. One-Click Installation & Reload
* Double-click **`install_RiderFeedHUD_Race.bat`** (Windows) or **`install_RiderFeedHUD_Race.command`** (Mac).
* In Sauce for Zwift, click your open HUD window and press **`Ctrl + R`** (or enable **Zwift 22.5K Race HUD v7** under Sauce Settings -> **Windows**).

## 2. Using the Top-Bar `POS` Box & 4 Tactical Cards
* **Top-Bar `POS` Box (`POS X / Total | C #Rank | B Wheel: -X.Xs`):**
  - Shows your overall position on the road, your position within Category C, and the gap to the nearest Category B wheel ahead of you.
* **Adjusting `1m` (`4.23`), `5m` (`3.40`), `20m` (`2.98`) W/kg or Course Distance (`22.5K` / `209m`):**
  - Click **`−` / `+`** directly on Cards 1, 2, or 3, or open **`⚙️` Settings** to type exact numbers for `1m`, `5m`, `20m`, Race `km`, or Elevation `m`.
* **Zeroing Distance & Elevation at the Start Line:**
  - Click **`0K`** on the far right of Card 4 as you leave the start pen to zero both the `22.5K` distance counter and the `209m` elevation counter.
  - Click **`Rst`** on Card 3 to refill your `1m`, `5m`, and `20m` power tanks to `100%`.

## 3. Reading Your Full-Row Ribbon & Rival Alerts
* **Your Row (`YOU`):** Scales across all 10 columns from **Green** (`< 2.24 W/kg`) -> **Orange** (`2.24–2.97 W/kg`) -> **Red + Gentle 2.0s Pulse** (`>= 2.98 W/kg`). You can switch the ribbon driver (`W/kg Power`, `W/kg + HR`, or `HR Only`) and pulse threshold (`>=20m`, `>=5m`, or `Off`) inside **`⚙️` Settings**.
* **Rival `RED 0-120s` & `Gap` Elastic Warnings:**
  - **`RED 0-120s`** flashes red when a rival enters Heart Rate Zone 5, 6, or 7 (`>= 90%` of their age-computed Max HR).
  - **`Gap`** shows live closing (`▲`) and opening (`▼`) arrows and flashes **Amber** when a wheel ahead (`-1.3s` to `-3.3s`) is about to snap the draft elastic.
