# Zwift 22.5K Race & 209m Climb HUD v2.0 (`RiderFeedHUD_22.5K_Race_Installer-v2.zip`)

Built specifically for tonight's **22.5 KM / 209m Climbing Zwift Race**, pre-calibrated with your ZwiftPower profile (**`57.6 kg`**, **`1m: 4.23 W/kg`**, **`5m: 3.40 W/kg`**, **`20m: 2.98 W/kg`**) in a clean racing layout (with no `LDR` or `SWP` buttons and no group-ride clutter).

---

## Key Race Features

1. **Option A (`W/kg` Power-Driven) Full-Row Scalable Ribbon (`Green Z1/2` -> `Orange Z3/4` -> `Red Z5/6/7`):**
   - Your active row (`M. Heady (YOU)`) changes colour and scales horizontally **all the way across all 9 columns** driven directly by your live `W/kg` relative to your `20m` (`2.98`), `5m` (`3.40`), and `1m` (`4.23`) targets:
     - **Zone 1 & 2 (`< 2.24 W/kg`):** Vibrant Emerald Green.
     - **Zone 3 & 4 (`2.24` to `2.97 W/kg`):** Warm Golden Amber to Vivid Orange.
     - **Zone 5, 6 & 7 (`>= 2.98 W/kg` through `3.40` & `4.23 W/kg`):** Crimson Red.
2. **Gentle Red Pulse at Threshold (`>= 2.98 W/kg`):**
   - Whenever your live power reaches or exceeds your 20-minute threshold (`2.98 W/kg`), your entire 9-column row gently breathes with a smooth 2.0-second red pulse so you can pace climbs without blowing up.
3. **Editable `1m` (`4.23`), `5m` (`3.40`), and `20m` (`2.98`) W/kg Countdown Tanks:**
   - **Card 1 (`1m: 4.23 W/kg` / `244W`):** Counts down from `1:00` (`60s`) when attacking or sprinting at `>= 4.23 W/kg`.
   - **Card 2 (`5m: 3.40 W/kg` / `196W`):** Counts down from `5:00` (`300s`) when climbing or bridging at `>= 3.40 W/kg`.
   - **Card 3 (`20m: 2.98 W/kg` / `172W`):** Counts down from `20:00` (`1200s`) when holding threshold at `>= 2.98 W/kg`.
   - All three values can be adjusted anytime via the `−` / `+` buttons on the top bar **or** typed directly inside the **`⚙️` Settings** cogwheel drawer.
4. **`22.5 KM` & `209m` Climbing Tracker + `57.6 kg` Lightweight Advantage:**
   - Tracks distance (`XX.X/22.5K`) and elevation (`⛰️ XXX/209m`), and calculates your live wattage savings vs heavier pack riders on every climb `>= +2.0%`.
5. **Clean Race Table (No `LDR` or `SWP` Buttons):**
   - Displays each racer's Category badge, Name, and `ATK` attack alert on the Light Blue **Nearby Athletes** table without leader or sweeper buttons.
