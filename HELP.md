# Master Unified RiderFeedHUD v22.1 — End-User Manual & WOW Leader Guide (Windows & Mac)

Click **`❓ Help ↗`** on Row 2 of the HUD at any time to open the interactive 4-tab Help & Visual Callout Guide window beside your live table, or click **`📏 0-100 ↗`** to pop out the Detachable `0 → 100` All-Categories Ribbon!

---

## 1. One-Click Installation (Windows & Mac)
* **Windows PC:** Double-click **`install_RiderFeedHUD_Unified.bat`**, then press **`Ctrl + R`** inside your Sauce HUD window (or enable **`Master Unified HUD v22.1`** under **Sauce Settings -> Windows**).
* **Mac (macOS):** Unzip the package, double-click **`install_RiderFeedHUD_Unified.command`** (if Gatekeeper prompts on first run, **Right-Click -> Open -> Open**), then press **`Cmd + R`** inside your Sauce HUD window.

---

## 2. 60-Second Pre-Ride Setup (Leading or Riding WOW 100K / 100 Miles)

1. **Pick Your Event Mode (Row 2 Left):**
   - Click **`👑 100K`** for the weekly 100K TTT (sets target to `100.0 km`, `KM / kph`, and enables `Auto: WOW Stg + Live` radar).
   - Click **`💯 100M/150`** for the monthly 100-Mile / Watopia 150 (sets target to `100.0 Miles` / `160.9 km`, switches units to `MI / mph`, enables `LP X/32` lap counter, and activates the `🔄 5.0K Lp` circuit filter).
   - Click **`🌿 40K`** for 40K Recovery (`2.40 W/kg` cap, `35 kph`) or **`🏁 Race`** for scratch races (`POS` box + `15s / 1m / 5m / 20m` power tanks).
2. **Select Your WOW Category (`A`–`E` on the Left Green Card):**
   - **Cat A (Red):** `3.5–4.5 W/kg` • `44 kph` (`27.3 mph`)
   - **Cat B (Green):** `2.7–3.3 W/kg` • `41 kph` (`25.5 mph`)
   - **Cat C (Blue):** `2.1–2.6 W/kg` • `39 kph` (`24.2 mph`)
   - **Cat D (Yellow):** `1.6–2.0 W/kg` • `37 kph` (`23.0 mph`)
   - **Cat E (Purple):** `1.2–1.5 W/kg` • `33 kph` (`20.5 mph`)
3. **Center-Lock Your Row (`🎯 YOU` on Row 2 Right):**
   - Click **`🎯 YOU`** so your row stays pinned dead-center in the middle of the table even when 800 riders are in the event (scroll your mouse wheel anytime for a 5-second free-scroll pause).
4. **Tag Co-Leaders & Sweeps:**
   - Click **`LDR`** on your Co-Leader's row (highlights their row in **Beacon Yellow** and tracks them in the left `🟡👑 LDR` card).
   - Click **`SWP`** on your Sweeper's row (highlights their row in **Vibrant Red** and tracks them in the middle `🧹 SWP` card).
5. **Hands-Free Start Pen Roll-Out (Zero Buttons to Click!):**
   - Just pedal out of the start pen when the gate drops! Zwift zeroes official event distance & clock automatically, and **`v22.1` automatically resets your Ride-Average W/kg (`Rst` -> `Auto✓`) as you roll past `0.05 mi` (`80m`)**.

## 3. Reading the `0 → 100` Vertical-Stem Ribbon (`📏 0-100 ↗`)

The `0 → 100` Ribbon is built directly above the Rider Table and can also be popped out into a larger standalone window via **`📏 0-100 ↗`** (or **`📏 Detach ↗`**):

- **Staggered Vertical Colour-Coded Leader Lines (Stems):**
  - **Above Tall Gold Line (`64px` up):** **`🟡 Cat D (YOU)`** — shows your current distance (`👑 D: 20.8M`) and elapsed ride clock (`⏱️ 54m 00s`).
  - **Above Short Green Line (`22px` up):** **`🟢 Cat B`** — shows Cat B's distance behind D (`B: -5.60mi`) and exact road time gap (`⏱️ -13m 00s`).
  - **Below Short Blue Line (`22px` down):** **`🔵 Cat C`** — shows Cat C's distance behind D (`C: -2.73mi`) and exact road time gap (`⏱️ -6m 41s`).
  - **Below Tall Red & Purple Lines (`64px` down):** **`🔴 Cat A`** (`A: -7.86mi` / `⏱️ -17m 11s`) and **`🟣 Cat E`** (`E: +2.88mi` / `⏱️ +7m 31s`).
- **5 Category Summary Cards (Detached Ribbon Window):**
  - Display each group's start clock, current distance, time gap to **Group D**, and **Group-to-Group time gap** (`Behind D`, `Behind C`, `Behind B`).
- **Text Size Zoom (`A−` / `A+`) & Practice Simulator (`🎛️ Sim`):**
  - Use **`A−` / `A+`** in the top-right bar to scale the ribbon text size, or use the bottom simulator controls (`1x / 15x / 45x` and scenario jump buttons) to preview the entire race choreography before ride day.

## 4. Customising Columns, Fonts, Colours & Stagger Times (`⚙️ Settings`)

- **Resize Any Column (`||` Splitters):** Drag the vertical splitter bar (`||`) on the right edge of any column header left or right (or double-click a splitter to toggle compact width).
- **Settings Drawer (`⚙️` Button in Top Bar):**
  - Adjust **Font Size (`px`)**, **UI Scale (`%`)**, **Background Opacity (`%`)**, and **256 Pantone Background Colours**.
  - Edit **WOW Staggered Start Times** (`E 13:30 | D 13:45 | C 13:55 | B 14:04 | A 14:11`) if an event uses a custom start schedule.
  - Toggle **Chaser Radar Mode** (`Auto: WOW Stg + Live` vs. `Live Nearby Only`) and **Radar Focus** (`Focus: ⇄ Behind` vs. `Ahead`).
- **Declutter Mode (`👁️ Clean` on Row 2):** Hides the top Pacer and Radar cards so only the Mode Strip, `0 → 100` Ribbon, and Rider Table remain visible.
