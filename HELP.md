## Which HUD Should I Enable in Sauce for Zwift?
1. **Sunday D WOW 100K TTT Group Ride (`2.00 W/kg` & `37 kph`):**
   - Folder: `Documents\SauceMods\RiderFeedHUD_100K_Leader`
   - Window Name: **WOW 100K Leader HUD v16 (Light Blue Screenshot | ⚙️ Settings | 2.0 • 37kph)**

   - Documentation: See `RiderFeedHUD_225K_Race/README.md` and `RiderFeedHUD_225K_Race/HELP.md`.

## One-Click GitHub Backup & SauceMods Sync
* Double-click **`sync_RiderFeedHUD_GitHub.bat`** anytime to verify all three folders in `Documents\SauceMods`, mirror them into `Documents\SauceMods\RiderFeedHUD`, and push the latest commit to `https://github.com/headmard-dev/RiderFeedHUD.git`.
