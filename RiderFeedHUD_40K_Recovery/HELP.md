# WOW 40K Recovery HUD — Quick User & Controls Guide

## 1. One-Click Installation & Reload
* Double-click **`install_RiderFeedHUD_40K.bat`** (Windows) or **`install_RiderFeedHUD_40K.command`** (Mac).
* In Sauce for Zwift, click your open HUD window and press **`Ctrl + R`** (or enable **WOW 40K Recovery HUD v16** under Sauce Settings -> **Windows**).

## 2. 40K Recovery Pacing (`Max 2.40 W/kg` on the Front & `35 kph`)
* Both the **`40K WKG`** (Top-Left) and **`40K`** (Top-Right) banners default to **`2.40 W/kg`** and **`35 kph`**.
* **Emerald Green (`FRONT OK` / `40K OK`):** Pace is at or below `2.40 W/kg`.
* **Flashing Red (`EASE!` / `OVER!`):** You have pushed over `2.40 W/kg` (`> 2.48 W/kg`)—ease slightly to keep the recovery group together.
* **Click `40K` (`XXK left`):** Zeroes the 40.0 km distance counter as you cross the start banner.
* **Tagging Leaders & Sweeps:** Click **`LDR`** on any row (or type in `Co-Ldr…`) to highlight a Leader/Co-Leader in **Beacon Yellow**; click **`SWP`** on any row (or type in `Sweep…`) to highlight a Sweep in **Vibrant Red**.

## 3. Windows View & `⚙️` Settings Controls (Top-Right of Black Ribbon)
* **`⚙️` Settings Cogwheel:** Adjust **Text Font Size**, **UI Scale**, **Background Opacity**, **256 Pantone Colours** (or **🟦 Screenshot Blue**), **Paged View (`📄 Paged`)**, and **Top Bar Pin (`📌`)**.
* **`⛶` Full (`F11`):** Toggles full-screen maximized mode and restores your previous window bounds.
* **`_` (Minimize):** Rolls up the rider table so only the top 40K & Sweep/Leader radar banners are shown.
* **`✕` Exit:** Closes the HUD window.
