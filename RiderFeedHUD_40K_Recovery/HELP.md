# WOW 40K Recovery HUD — Quick User & Controls Guide

## 1. Windows View & Exit Controls (Top-Right of Black Ribbon)

* **`📄 Paged`:** Toggles Paged View on/off. When active, a navigation bar appears at the bottom of the table with:
  - **`◀ Prev` / `Next ▶`** (or `PageUp` / `PageDown` keys) to flip between Front Pack and Tail/Sweeps.
  - **`⟳ Auto-Flip: ON (6s)`:** Hands-free mode that flips between pages every 6 seconds during the ride.
  - **`8 / Page`:** Cycles between `6`, `8`, and `10` riders per page.
* **`⛶ Full` (`❐ Restore` or `F11`):** Toggles full-screen maximized mode and restores your previous window bounds.
* **`—` (`▢ Expand`):** Rolls up the rider table so only the top 40K & Sweep/Leader radar banners are shown.
* **`✕ Exit`:** Closes the HUD window.

## 2. 40K Recovery Pacing (`Max 2.40 W/kg` on the Front)
* Both the **`40K WKG`** (Top-Left) and **`40K`** (Top-Right) banners default to **`2.40 W/kg`**.
* **Green (`FRONT OK` / `40K OK`):** Pace is at or below `2.40 W/kg`.
* **Flashing Red (`EASE!` / `OVER!`):** You have pushed over `2.40 W/kg` (`> 2.48 W/kg`)—ease slightly to keep the recovery group together.
